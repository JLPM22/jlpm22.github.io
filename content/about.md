**Generative AI · Character Animation · Robotics · Computer Graphics · XR/VR**

Hello! I am an **AI and Computer Graphics researcher** specializing in Generative AI, Human Motion Models, and HCI, and I am now focused on applying my research to robotics. I am currently a Postdoctoral Researcher at the [Max Planck Institute for Informatics](https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/visual-computing-and-artificial-intelligence). Recently, I earned my Ph.D. *Cum Laude* from [UPC](https://www.upc.edu/en) Barcelona, where my dissertation was recognized with the **Best PhD Award by the Eurographics Spanish Section**.

My background spans academia, Big Tech, and startups. As a Research Scientist Intern at **Meta**, I developed a scalable AI motion style transfer framework that progressed from prototype to production. Additionally, I built computer vision-based motion capture pipelines for Hyper Online, a **Y Combinator startup**.

My research is published in premier venues, including **SIGGRAPH, SIGGRAPH Asia, Eurographics, IEEE VR, and CHI**, and is widely adopted by the community. I actively maintain an open-source **Motion Matching** engine for Unity with over 500 GitHub stars, and I have grown a Spanish-language Game Dev YouTube channel to over 120,000 subscribers and 9 million views.
